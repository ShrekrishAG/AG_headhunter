# Role brief: Roofing General / Sales Manager

**Job posting:** [IdealTraits — Accord Group Inc.](https://app.idealtraits.com/career/Accord-Group-Inc./358151vie)  
**Location:** 1305 E Waterman St., Wichita, KS 67211 (on-site / field — not remote)  
**Type:** Full-time · Sales Management  
**Compensation path:** Successful GMs / Managing Partners earn **$200K+** annually

## Overview

Lead a location as a **Managing Partner / General Manager** for Accord Group exterior restoration (roofing, siding, gutters). This is hands-on leadership: hiring, coaching, sales strategy, operations, and P&L ownership — not a desk-only role.

You run your own location while being mentored toward multi-unit leadership. High-pressure, results-driven environment with heavy local travel (up to ~70%).

## Career path

1. **MIT (Manager in Training) → General Manager** — Lead a team; manage team projects; prepare for multi-unit leadership  
2. **General Manager / Managing Partner** — Own operations, scale the business, earn at the top end of the compensation path

## Who you are

- Hungry to grow personally and professionally  
- Strong communicator with a competitive edge  
- Willing to work hard, be coached, and lead by example  
- Committed to a long-term leadership path  

## Core responsibilities

1. **Financial responsibility** — Manage revenue and expenses to maximize project profitability for the team  
2. **Revenue attainment** — Meet or exceed sales goals via sales activity, territory management, and strategic hiring  
3. **Lead, manage & hold accountable** — Coach and develop the team; set expectations; have difficult conversations when needed  
4. **Remarkable experiences** — Clear expectations, strong communication, consistent results for customers and teammates  
5. **Ultimate problem resolution** — Own issues related to work quality, customer experience, and collections at the location  

## Qualifications

- **3+ years** of sales and/or sales management experience (minimum bar — more years is fine; do **not** treat 5 years as a ceiling or preference band)
- Proven track record recruiting, hiring, training, leading, and developing a direct sales team
- Ability to lead in a high-pressure, fast-paced, results-driven environment
- Intermediate computer skills; web/mobile apps; CRM preferred
- Open to travel (heavy local; possible extended stays)
- Physical / field capability: ladders, roof inspections, lifting construction materials as needed

## Benefits

Health, dental, vision, life insurance, retirement plan, hands-on training, career growth opportunities
