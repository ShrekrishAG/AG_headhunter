# Scorecard: Roofing General / Sales Manager (Resume Rubric)

**Total score: 100 points** · Score each category based **only on evidence visible in the resume**.

**Role:** General Manager / Sales Manager / Managing Partner (location leadership)  
**Posting:** [IdealTraits](https://app.idealtraits.com/career/Accord-Group-Inc./358151vie)

---

## 1. Sales & Revenue Leadership — 25 points

| Criteria | Points |
|----------|--------|
| 3+ years sales and/or sales management (home services, roofing, construction, field sales preferred) | 10 |
| Evidence of meeting or exceeding team or personal revenue / sales goals | 8 |
| Territory, pipeline, or book-of-business ownership with measurable outcomes | 7 |

**Strong indicators:** “Grew location revenue X%,” “exceeded team quota,” “managed $X book,” “top branch / top team.”

---

## 2. People Leadership (Hire · Coach · Accountable) — 25 points

| Criteria | Points |
|----------|--------|
| Proven recruiting, hiring, and onboarding of sales or field staff | 8 |
| Training, coaching, or developing a direct-report team | 8 |
| Holding people accountable to results (performance management, standards, tough conversations) | 5 |
| Team size / span of control clearly stated (e.g. led 5–20+ people) | 4 |

**Strong indicators:** “Built team from X to Y,” “hired and trained N reps,” “performance-managed underperformers,” “promoted from within.”

---

## 3. Operations & P&L Ownership — 20 points

| Criteria | Points |
|----------|--------|
| Owned or contributed to location / branch / unit P&L, budget, or profitability | 8 |
| Managed expenses, margins, or project profitability (not only top-line sales) | 6 |
| Multi-function ownership (sales + ops, production handoff, scheduling, collections touchpoints) | 6 |

**Strong indicators:** “P&L responsibility,” “margin improvement,” “controlled costs,” “branch manager,” “general manager,” “operations manager.”

---

## 4. Customer Experience & Problem Resolution — 15 points

| Criteria | Points |
|----------|--------|
| Owned customer escalations, quality issues, or service recovery | 6 |
| Collections, warranty, or post-sale issue resolution experience | 4 |
| Clear evidence of setting expectations and delivering consistent customer outcomes | 5 |

**Strong indicators:** “Resolved escalations,” “improved CSAT / reviews,” “owned collections,” “quality control,” “customer experience.”

---

## 5. Tools, Systems & Execution Discipline — 10 points

| Criteria | Points |
|----------|--------|
| CRM and sales-ops tooling (JobNimbus, AccuLynx, Salesforce, HubSpot, ServiceTitan, etc.) | 4 |
| Reporting, forecasting, dashboards, or activity scorecards | 3 |
| Comfort with mobile / field tech and process-driven execution | 3 |

---

## 6. Culture Fit & Leadership Readiness — 5 points

| Criteria | Points |
|----------|--------|
| Signals of coachability, ownership, and leading by example in high-pace environments | 3 |
| Willingness / history of field presence, travel, or hands-on leadership (not purely remote desk) | 2 |

---

## 7. Red Flags / Risk Factors — subtract up to 15 points

| Red flag | Deduction |
|----------|-----------|
| No people-management or team-leadership evidence for a GM-level claim | −5 to −8 |
| Pure individual contributor sales only, with no coaching / hiring story | −3 to −5 |
| Frequent short stints (&lt;12 months) without explanation | −2 to −4 |
| Inflated titles with no measurable outcomes | −2 to −4 |
| Unexplained employment gaps or conflicting dates | −2 to −3 |
| Only corporate / desk leadership with zero field or customer-facing exposure (for this role) | −2 to −4 |

Do **not** deduct for missing IdealTraits assessment / video answers — those are later in the process.

---

## Recommendation bands

| Total | Recommendation |
|-------|----------------|
| **85–100** | Strong interview — prioritize for leadership conversation |
| **70–84** | Good candidate — solid GM / sales-manager signal |
| **60–69** | Phone screen — mixed evidence; probe P&L + hiring depth |
| **&lt;60** | Likely not a fit — insufficient leadership / revenue ownership evidence |

**Qualified for pipeline:** total **≥ 70** (higher bar than Project Sales Rep — this is a leadership hire).

---

## Scoring notes for raters

- Weight **people leadership + revenue ownership** heavily — the IdealTraits posting centers hiring, coaching, accountability, and sales goals.  
- Prefer **location / branch / team** evidence over pure corporate staff roles.  
- Roofing / home services / storm restoration / construction sales management is a plus, not a hard requirement if leadership evidence is strong.  
- Physical / ladder requirements are job conditions — do not score them from the resume unless relevant experience is present.
