# AI Recruiting Search Instructions
## General Manager / Market Partner

Source: Accord recruiting search playbook (GM). Use this when filtering and scoring candidates.

### Objective

Build a pipeline of high-quality external candidates for **General Manager / Market Partner**. This is **not** a traditional office management role. We want entrepreneurial leaders who **recruit, train, coach, personally sell**, and build high-performing **commission-based** sales organizations.

### Years of experience

- Require evidence of **3+ years** of sales and/or sales management experience.
- There is **no 5-year upper preference** — do not de-prioritize candidates solely for having more than 5 years.
- Under 3 years of relevant sales/sales-management evidence should score poorly on tenure criteria.

### Target industries (prioritize)

Roofing Restoration, Solar, Pest Control, Home Improvement, Windows, Siding, Gutters, Foundation Repair, HVAC, Garage Doors, Exterior Remodeling, Building Materials, Industrial Sales, Construction Equipment, Logistics.

### Target titles (search / positive signals)

Sales Manager, Branch Manager, Market Manager, General Manager, Operating Partner, Regional Sales Manager, District Manager, Area Sales Manager, Team Leader, Territory Manager, Branch Director, and similar leadership roles.

### Behavioral keywords (positive signals)

recruited salespeople, built sales team, hired, sales coaching, field coaching, ride-alongs, outside sales, door-to-door, canvassing, prospecting, commission sales, territory expansion, market development, launched new branch, opened market, scaled organization, P&L responsibility, startup, builder, player-coach, ownership mentality, leadership development.

### Ideal profile (AI search prompt)

Find candidates who have successfully **built, recruited, trained, and led commission-based outside sales organizations**. Prioritize leaders who remain active in **field sales** and coach through **ride-alongs** rather than managing exclusively from an office. Strong preference for experience in roofing restoration, solar, pest control, home improvement, HVAC, siding, windows, or other direct-sales industries. Candidates should demonstrate recruiting, hiring, coaching, territory expansion, market development, P&L ownership, and entrepreneurial leadership.

### De-prioritize / avoid

- Primarily **inside sales**, **retail management**, **customer service**, or **corporate administration**
- Careers that are primarily **administrative or office-based** with little field / commission / recruiting evidence

### Strong-candidate filters

Strong candidates have hired and developed teams, led by example in the field, succeeded in commission environments, built new markets or branches, and developed future leaders.

### Success standard

We are hiring **builders**, not desk managers. The ideal candidate can enter a new market, recruit a team, knock doors alongside them, create accountability, and grow a profitable business with an ownership mindset.
